Oxford Standard Chapter 1

Operasi Pada Pecahan (Fractions)

Aturan penjumlahan, pengurangan, perkalian, dan pembagian pecahan biasa serta campuran.

1 Penjumlahan & Pengurangan Pecahan (Addition & Subtraction)

Aturan Utama: Untuk menjumlahkan atau mengurangkan pecahan, penyebut (denominator) harus disamakan terlebih dahulu menggunakan Kelipatan Persekutuan Terkecil (KPK / LCM).

Langkah Kerja:

  1. Ubah pecahan campuran menjadi pecahan biasa (jika ada).
  2. Cari KPK penyebut untuk menyamakan penyebut.
  3. Sesuaikan pembilang (numerator) sebanding.
  4. Jumlahkan/kurangkan pembilang saja, penyebut tetap.
  5. Sederhanakan pecahan akhir (simplest form).

Contoh Soal (Example):

Hitung: \(\frac{3}{8} + \frac{5}{12}\)

KPK dari 8 dan 12 adalah 24.
\(\frac{3 \times 3}{8 \times 3} + \frac{5 \times 2}{12 \times 2} = \frac{9}{24} + \frac{10}{24} = \frac{19}{24}\)

2 Perkalian Pecahan (Multiplication of Fractions)

Aturan Utama: Kalikan pembilang dengan pembilang, dan penyebut dengan penyebut. TIDAK PERLU menyamakan penyebut!

Tips Metode Coret (Cross-Simplification / Cancelling):

Sederhanakan faktor persekutuan antara pembilang mana saja dengan penyebut mana saja sebelum dikalikan untuk mempermudah hitungan!

\(\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \times c}{b \times d}\)

3 Pembagian Pecahan: Metode Langsung & Flip (KCF Method)

Dalam standar Oxford, pembagian pecahan dipahami sebagai "Berapa banyak bagian \(\frac{c}{d}\) di dalam \(\frac{a}{b}\)".

Metode 1: Flip & Multiply (KCF Rule)

Keep, Change, Flip: Simpan pecahan 1, Ubah \(\div\) jadi \(\times\), Balik pecahan 2 (reciprocal).

$$\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c} = \frac{ad}{bc}$$

Metode 2: Pembagian Langsung (Direct Division)

Bisa dilakukan langsung jika pembilang habis dibagi pembilang dan penyebut habis dibagi penyebut.

$$\frac{8}{15} \div \frac{2}{3} = \frac{8 \div 2}{15 \div 3} = \frac{4}{5}$$
Oxford Standard Chapter 2

Operasi Desimal (Decimals Mastery)

Penjumlahan, pengurangan, perkalian, pembagian, serta trik pengerjaan cepat (Mental & Written Methods).

1 Penjumlahan & Pengurangan Desimal

Aturan Emas: Tye-up / Sejajarkan Tanda Koma (Decimal Point) secara vertikal.

Tambahkan angka \(0\) bayangan di kanan jika jumlah digit di belakang koma tidak sama (placeholder zeroes).

  13.700
- 8.140
   5.560

2 Perkalian Desimal (Multiplication Rules & Shortcuts)

Kasus A

Desimal \(\times\) Bilangan Bulat

Abaikan koma terlebih dahulu, lakukan perkalian bersusun biasa, lalu kembalikan koma sejumlah angka desimal awal.

Contoh: \(2.13 \times 4\)

1. Hitung \(213 \times 4 = 852\)

2. Ada 2 angka di belakang koma (\(2.13\)).

3. Hasil akhir: \(8.52\)

Kasus B

Desimal \(\times\) Desimal

Jumlahkan total tempat desimal (decimal places) dari kedua bilangan!

Contoh: \(2.13 \times 4.2\)

1. Hitung \(213 \times 42 = 8946\)

2. Total desimal: \(2.13\) (2 angka) + \(4.2\) (1 angka) = 3 angka.

3. Geser koma 3 tempat ke kiri: \(8.946\)

3 Pembagian Desimal (Division Rules & Hacks)

Desimal \(\div\) Bilangan Bulat

Gunakan pembagian bersusun (long division / busur). Naikkan titik koma lurus tepat ke atas ke tempat hasil.

Contoh: \(14.4 \div 6 = 2.4\)

Desimal \(\div\) Desimal (Trik Penting)

Langkah Utama: Ubah pembagi (divisor) menjadi bilangan bulat dengan mengalikan kelipatan 10 (\(10, 100, 1000\)). Kalikan bilangan yang dibagi dengan kelipatan yang sama!

Hitung: \(3.496 \div 2.3\)

1. Ubah \(2.3\) jadi bulat \(\rightarrow 2.3 \times 10 = 23\).

2. Kalikan juga \(3.496 \times 10 = 34.96\).

3. Sekarang hitung: \(34.96 \div 23 = \mathbf{1.52}\)

Oxford Exam Strategy: Fact-Based Calculations

Di ujian Oxford, sering ada soal berpola: "Use the fact that \(24 \times 35 = 840\) to answer..."
Trik: Jangan menghitung ulang dari nol! Gunakan pergeseran tempat nilai desimal.

\(24 \times 0.35 = \mathbf{8.4}\)
(geser koma 2 kali)
\(840 \div 3.5 = \mathbf{240}\)
(\(8400 \div 35\))
\(0.24 \times 3.5 = \mathbf{0.84}\)
(2+1 = 3 desimal)

Persentase & Konversi (Percentages)

Konversi F-D-P (Fraction-Decimal-Percentage) dan perhitungan persentase kuantitas.

1 Segitiga Konversi (F-D-P Conversion Trinity)

Pecahan \(\rightarrow\) Desimal / Persen

Bagi pembilang dengan penyebut, atau ubah penyebut ke 100.

\(\frac{3}{5} = \frac{60}{100} = 0.60 = 60\%\)

Desimal \(\rightarrow\) Pecahan (Simplest Form)

Tulis sebagai pecahan per 10/100/1000 lalu sederhanakan.

\(0.85 = \frac{85}{100} \div \frac{5}{5} = \frac{17}{20}\)

Persen \(\rightarrow\) Kuantitas (Amount)

Ubah persen ke desimal/pecahan lalu kalikan nilai total.

\(20\% \text{ dari } £12,000 = 0.2 \times 12000 = £2400\)

Questions in English
Question 1: Fact-Based Money Problem [3 Marks]

Use the fact that 18 × 45 = 810 to answer the question below:

Lucas buys 18 custom notebooks for his club. Each notebook costs $0.45. He pays the cashier with a $20 note. Work out how much change Lucas should receive. Show all your working.

Langkah Pengerjaan Detail:

  1. Hitung Total Harga Beli: Menggunakan fakta \(18 \times 45 = 810\).
    Karena harganya \(\$0.45\) (ada 2 angka di belakang koma), maka:
    \(\text{Total Cost} = 18 \times \$0.45 = \mathbf{\$8.10}\).
  2. Hitung Uang Kembalian (Change):
    \(\text{Change} = \$20.00 - \$8.10 = \mathbf{\$11.90}\).

Final Answer: Lucas should get $11.90 change.

Question 2: Error Analysis & BIDMAS / BODMAS [2 Marks]

Hannah works out 4.2 + 3.6 × 0.4 and gets the answer 3.12.

Hannah's answer is wrong. Explain the mistake Hannah has made and find the correct answer.

Analisis Kesalahan & Urutan Operasi (BODMAS):

Penjelasan Kesalahan (Explanation): Hannah melakukan penjumlahan terlebih dahulu (\(4.2 + 3.6 = 7.8\)) baru dikalikan \(0.4\). Ini salah karena menurut aturan hirarki operasi (BODMAS/BIDMAS), perkalian harus dikerjakan sebelum penjumlahan.

Langkah Pembetulan yang Benar:

1. Kerjakan perkalian dulu: \(3.6 \times 0.4 = 1.44\)

2. Lalu tambahkan: \(4.2 + 1.44 = \mathbf{5.64}\)

Correct Answer: 5.64

Question 3: Pie Chart Sector & Unknown Fraction [4 Marks]

A survey was conducted on students' favorite sports. The fractions for each sport are given below:

Football: \(\frac{1}{3}\)
Basketball: \(\frac{1}{4}\)
Swimming: \(\frac{1}{6}\)
Tennis: \(\frac{1}{8}\)
Badminton: [ Remaining ]
  1. Work out the fraction of students who chose Badminton. Give your fraction in its simplest form.
  2. Calculate the angle of the sector representing Badminton in a pie chart.

Langkah Pengerjaan Pie Chart Angle:

(a) Mencari Pecahan Sisa (Badminton):

Total satu lingkaran penuh = 1.

KPK dari penyebut (\(3, 4, 6, 8\)) adalah 24.

\(\text{Jumlah Olahraga Lain} = \frac{8}{24} + \frac{6}{24} + \frac{4}{24} + \frac{3}{24} = \frac{21}{24}\)

\(\text{Pecahan Badminton} = 1 - \frac{21}{24} = \frac{3}{24} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{8}}\)

(b) Menghitung Sudut Sektor (Sector Angle):

Total sudut satu lingkaran penuh = \(360^\circ\).

\(\text{Sudut Badminton} = \frac{1}{8} \times 360^\circ = \mathbf{45^\circ}\)

Final Answers: (a) 1/8   (b) 45°

Question 4: Real-world Tax & Percentage [4 Marks]

Amira earns $24,000 per year. The first $7,500 of her income is untaxed (tax-free). The remaining income is taxed at a rate of 15%.

  1. How much tax does Amira pay per year?
  2. How much tax does Amira pay per month?
  3. What overall percentage of her total salary is paid as tax? Give your answer to 1 decimal place.

Langkah Pengerjaan Pajak & Persentase:

(a) Pajak per Tahun:

Penghasilan kena pajak = \(\$24,000 - \$7,500 = \$16,500\)

\(\text{Pajak Tahunan} = 15\% \times 16,500 = 0.15 \times 16,500 = \mathbf{\$2,475}\)

(b) Pajak per Bulan:

\(\text{Pajak Bulanan} = \frac{\$2,475}{12 \text{ bulan}} = \mathbf{\$206.25}\)

(c) Persentase Total Pajak terhadap Gaji Utuh:

\(\text{Persentase} = \left( \frac{2475}{24000} \right) \times 100\% = 10.3125\% \approx \mathbf{10.3\%}\)

Final Answers: (a) $2475   (b) $206.25   (c) 10.3%

Question 5: Fundraising & Target Verification [4 Marks]

School Science Club Fundraising Project

The target for the fundraising event is $3,500.

  • Week 1: Students raised $1,120.40 selling bake sale items.
  • Week 2: A sponsored run raised \(\frac{2}{5}\) of the total target amount.

At the end of Week 2, the coordinator says: "We have already reached more than 70% of our target."

Is the coordinator's statement correct? Explain by showing all your calculations.

Pembuktian Kebenaran Pernyataan (Verifikasi):

Langkah 1: Hitung Hasil Minggu 2:

\(\text{Week 2} = \frac{2}{5} \times \$3,500 = 2 \times \$700 = \$1,400\)

Langkah 2: Hitung Total Uang Terkumpul (Week 1 + Week 2):

\(\text{Total} = \$1,120.40 + \$1,400.00 = \mathbf{\$2,520.40}\)

Langkah 3: Hitung Nilai Target 70%:

\(70\% \text{ dari } \$3,500 = 0.70 \times 3500 = \mathbf{\$2,450}\)

Langkah 4: Bandingkan & Ambil Kesimpulan:

Karena \(\$2,520.40 > \$2,450\), maka uang yang terkumpul memang lebih dari 70% dari target.

Final Answer: Yes, the coordinator is CORRECT because $2,520.40 is greater than $2,450 (which is 70% of the target).
